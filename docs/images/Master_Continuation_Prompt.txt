Research Objective

Compare three VideoQA approaches:

Baseline Vision-Language Model
Pretrained Representation Learning
Self-Supervised Representation Learning

The goal is to determine whether self-supervised autoencoder video representations can approach or exceed pretrained representations when used for downstream multiple-choice VideoQA.
